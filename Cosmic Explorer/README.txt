Cosmic Explorer

HOW TO RUN
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open a terminal inside it and run:   python3 -m http.server 8000
3. Open  http://localhost:8000  in Chrome.
(Double-clicking index.html will not load the textures. Use a local server, or VS Code "Live Server".)
Internet is needed because three.js and the fonts load from a CDN.

CONTROLS
Solar system:  scroll to bring it forward, drag to look from any side (left, right, top, bottom), click a planet to fly to it
Planet page:   scroll or pinch to zoom, scroll out (or Esc, or the All planets button) to go back, drag to rotate, Left/Right arrows for next planet

CREDIT (keep this on the website)
Planet textures: Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0 - https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures
Planet facts: NASA Planetary Fact Sheet
